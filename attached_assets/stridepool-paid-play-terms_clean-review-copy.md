## 1. Eligibility

1.1 You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher).

1.2 You must be physically located in, and a resident of, a jurisdiction where Paid Challenges are permitted. Paid Challenges are not available in: Arizona, Arkansas, Delaware, Louisiana, Montana, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, and Vermont. We may change this list at any time.

1.3 You may hold only one account. Entering a Paid Challenge from more than one account is prohibited.

1.4 You must complete identity verification (see Section 9) before your first Paid Challenge, or before your first payout, as we determine.

1.5 StridePool employees, contractors, and their immediate household members may not enter Paid Challenges unless we state otherwise in writing.

## 2. Nature of Paid Challenges (Skill-Based Contests)

2.1 Paid Challenges are contests of physical activity. Outcomes are determined solely by the step counts and activity data you generate and that are verified under these terms. Outcomes do not depend on chance, a random draw, or the outcome of any sporting event.

2.2 StridePool is not a casino, sportsbook, or lottery, and Paid Challenges are not gambling products.

2.3 Participation in a Paid Challenge does not guarantee you will win or recover your entry fee.

## 3. How a Paid Challenge Works

3.1 **Challenge rules.** Each Paid Challenge displays, before you enter: the challenge entry fee, the separate platform fee and total payment, the daily step goal (or other goal), the duration, the start and end dates and times (with time zone), the format (for example, elimination or finish-the-goal), the absence of an automatic grace-day allowance, the maximum and minimum number of participants, and the payout rules.

3.2 **Entry.** Your entry is complete when your entry fee is successfully processed and the challenge screen shows you as joined. You may leave a challenge and receive a refund only before the join deadline shown on the challenge, if it allows leaving.

3.3 **Goals.** Goals may be set by StridePool or, where the challenge allows, personalized to you at entry. Once a challenge starts, your goal is locked and cannot be changed.

3.4 **Elimination format.** In elimination challenges, a missed daily goal on a completed challenge day causes elimination under the challenge's verification rules. Your live status shows "Eliminated," and your challenge entry fee stays in the pool. There is no automatic grace-day allowance.

3.5 **No automatic grace days.** Paid Challenges do not provide a grace-day allowance. Where the challenge requires a daily goal, you must meet that goal on every challenge day to qualify as a Finisher. Injury-related recovery and support review are separate processes; they do not create a grace-day allowance or automatically excuse a missed goal. Payment-provider delivery timing is also separate from challenge qualification.

3.6 **Completion.** You are a "Finisher" if you meet every requirement of the challenge through its end under these terms and your data passes verification.

## 4. The Prize Pool and Payouts

4.1 **Pool.** The prize pool for a Paid Challenge is made up of the creator-set challenge entry fees contributed by participants in that challenge. The separate platform fees described in Section 5 are charged in addition to those contributions, do not enter the prize pool, and are not deducted from the prize pool.

4.2 **Split.** At the end of the challenge, the pool is divided equally among all Finishers, unless the challenge states a different payout structure before you enter.

4.3 **Rounding.** Prize pools and payouts are calculated in integer USD cents. For equal-split challenges, each Finisher receives the whole-cent base share, and any remaining whole cents are distributed among qualifying Finishers by the settlement allocator. For ranking challenges, the displayed payout structure is applied in whole cents, with remaining cents allocated deterministically among the eligible winners. Distributed payouts must total the prize pool; StridePool does not retain a rounding remainder from the pool.

4.4 **No-Finisher Refund.** If no participant finishes a Paid Challenge, every participant who was not disqualified under Section 8 receives a full refund of their entry fee. Refunds are issued to your original payment method or your StridePool wallet balance, as we choose.

4.5 **Cancelled or under-filled challenges.** If a challenge is cancelled, or does not reach its minimum number of participants by its start time, every entry fee is refunded in full.

4.6 **Timing.** Settlement begins after the challenge ends and after verification windows close (see Section 7). We aim to finalize payouts within 5 business days of challenge end. Payout timing to your bank or card also depends on our payment provider and your financial institution.

4.7 **Settlement is final once completed.** A challenge moves through the states open, settling, and settled. Once a challenge is marked "settled," results are final except as stated in Section 8 (fraud) and Section 10 (disputes).

## 5. Fees

5.1 **Separate platform fee.** In addition to the creator-set challenge entry fee, Free members pay a $2 platform fee per paid entry and Pro members pay a $1 platform fee per paid entry, according to their effective membership at checkout. The platform fee is revenue paid to StridePool, not a contribution to the prize pool. StridePool does not deduct that platform fee from the prize pool.

5.2 **Payment processing and total payment.** Stripe processes your payment. The total shown before checkout is the challenge entry fee plus the separate platform fee in Section 5.1. StridePool absorbs the payment-processing fees charged to it by Stripe; no additional Stripe processing surcharge is added to that disclosed total.

5.3 **Third-party charges.** Your bank, card issuer, or mobile carrier may charge you fees. We are not responsible for those.

## 6. Payments and Wallet

6.1 **Payment provider.** Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. and its affiliates. By paying, you also agree to the provider's terms.

6.2 **Wallet balance.** Winnings and refunds may be credited to your StridePool wallet. Wallet balances are a record of amounts owed to you. They are not a bank account, are not insured by the FDIC or any government agency, and do not earn interest. Free-play balances and credits are kept separate from paid balances and cannot be withdrawn as cash.

6.3 **Withdrawals.** You may withdraw eligible available wallet balances to a verified payout method, subject to identity verification, a minimum withdrawal amount of $20, and the timing in Section 4.6. You may choose an amount of at least $20, up to your available balance. Pending funds do not count toward the available balance.

6.4 **Chargebacks.** If you file a chargeback or payment dispute for an entry fee, we may disqualify you from the challenge, suspend your account, and recover any winnings or credits associated with it. We may contest chargebacks that we believe are improper.

6.5 **Taxes.** You are responsible for any taxes on your winnings. We may collect tax information and report winnings to tax authorities where the law requires, including issuing tax forms. You agree to provide accurate tax information on request, and we may withhold payouts until you do.

6.6 **Unclaimed funds.** If you do not provide required information or your account is inactive for 24 months, we may be required to treat remaining balances as unclaimed property under applicable law.

## 7. Activity Data and Verification

7.1 **Data sources.** Steps and activity are collected from the sources you connect, such as Apple Health (HealthKit), Health Connect on Android, or other supported services and devices. You must grant the required permissions for your steps to count.

7.2 **Accuracy.** Step counts come from third-party devices and platforms. We do not guarantee their accuracy. Sync delays, device errors, and permission changes can affect your counts, and you are responsible for keeping your device charged, synced, and permitted.

7.3 **Source rules.** Only data that we accept as coming from a supported, unmodified device or health platform counts. Manually entered steps, steps from sources we do not support, and steps we determine were not generated by your own walking or running do not count.

7.4 **Sync window.** Daily steps must sync to StridePool within 24 hours after each day ends to count for that day. Data that arrives later may be disregarded.

7.5 **Verification.** We may run automated and manual checks on your data, including checks for abnormal patterns, impossible step rates, duplicate or conflicting sources, mock location or device tampering signals, and mismatches across sources. We may require additional proof, such as a screenshot of your health app, a device check, or a short verification activity.

7.6 **Review window.** After a challenge ends, we hold results for a review period of up to 24 hours before settlement so that verification can complete.

- use emulators, rooted or jailbroken devices for tampering, spoofed location, or automation;
- operate multiple accounts, or collude with other participants;
- exploit bugs or errors in StridePool;
- provide false identity, location, or payment information;
- harass other participants or staff.

8.2 **Consequences.** If we determine, in our reasonable discretion, that you violated this section, we may: remove your steps, disqualify you from the challenge, forfeit your entry fee into the pool or refund it at our discretion, reverse payouts, claw back winnings, suspend or close your account, and report conduct to authorities or payment providers where appropriate.

8.3 **Disqualified participants are not Finishers.** A disqualified participant is excluded from the payout split and from any no-finisher refund, unless we decide otherwise in writing.

8.4 **Reversal after settlement.** If fraud is discovered after a challenge is settled, we may recalculate the results and redistribute funds among the remaining Finishers, or recover funds from the violating account.

8.5 **Admin overrides.** StridePool administrators may manually adjust results in cases of verified technical error, outage, or data-source failure. Any manual override is logged and may be reviewed in a dispute under Section 10.
