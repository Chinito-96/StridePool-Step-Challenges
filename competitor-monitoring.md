## 30-minute monthly ritual

1. Check new App Store / Play Store reviews for tracking, payout, fee, and support complaints.
2. Check each competitor’s homepage and pricing or rewards language.
3. Scan announcements and social posts for new challenge formats, integrations, and reward models.
4. Compare onboarding friction, fee disclosure, challenge creation, social features, and device support.
5. Update the benchmark and log changes that affect StridePool’s roadmap.
