---
name: Ranking challenge podium rules
description: Durable settlement rules for StridePool total-steps ranking challenges.
---

Ranking challenges have no daily target or missed-day elimination. Rank contestants by total verified steps within the configured challenge dates. First, second, and third receive 60%, 25%, and 15% of the pot. Creator-paid daily-goal challenges are separate: they require the configured target on every day and split the pool equally among all finishers.

**Why:** Contestants need a predictable podium format that does not reuse daily-goal qualification, while creator goal challenges need an explicit all-days finish line.

**How to apply:** Tied contestants split the combined shares of the places they occupy. If fewer than three contestants enter, distribute the entire pot among the available places. Use stable entry ordering for indivisible-cent remainders.
