---
name: Ranking challenge podium rules
description: Durable settlement rules for StridePool total-steps ranking challenges.
---

Ranking challenges have no daily target or missed-day elimination. Rank contestants by total verified steps within the configured challenge dates. First, second, and third receive 50%, 30%, and 20% of the pot.

**Why:** Contestants need a predictable podium format that does not reuse daily-goal qualification, while existing goal and elimination settlement behavior must remain unchanged.

**How to apply:** Tied contestants split the combined shares of the places they occupy. If fewer than three contestants enter, distribute the entire pot among the available places. Use stable entry ordering for indivisible-cent remainders.
