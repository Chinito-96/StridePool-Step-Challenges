---
name: Membership pricing
description: The durable Free and Pro pricing and entitlement rules for challenge participation.
---

Free members pay a fixed $2 contribution per joined challenge and may have one active challenge. Pro costs $5/month, charges a fixed $1 contribution per joined challenge, allows five active challenges, and allows one active challenge created by that member. A Pro creator must pay the same $1 contribution and occupies one of the five participation slots.

**Why:** The fixed contribution replaces creator-selected entry amounts. It is not an added platform fee or a deduction from another entry amount.

**How to apply:** Enforce plan limits and prices on the server at checkout and provider fulfillment. Grant Pro only from signed subscription events, and never activate a creator as a participant before their entry payment succeeds.
