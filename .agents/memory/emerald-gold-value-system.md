---
name: Emerald-gold value system
description: The product rule for using emerald, warm cream, charcoal, and gold/brass in StridePool value-oriented screens.
---

Use deep emerald for primary surfaces and navigation, warm cream for cards and page backgrounds, and deep charcoal for general text. Reserve bright metallic gold for confirmed value states: balances, prize pools, payout amounts, streaks, progress, and on-track-to-win indicators. Gold is not a generic status, navigation, or decoration color.

**Why:** Gold used as ordinary text on warm cream does not maintain sufficient contrast, while gold on emerald makes value-related information easy to scan without turning the whole interface into an alert state.

**How to apply:** Use bright gold on emerald surfaces; use a darker brass text token for value amounts on cream; keep generic joined/status badges and non-value icons in emerald, muted green, cream, or charcoal.
