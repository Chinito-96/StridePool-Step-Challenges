---
name: Free community identity boundary
description: Product boundary for anonymous free participation and authenticated social features
---

Anonymous walkers may persist membership in free community challenges through a browser-scoped identity, but social graph features and personal streak/step history require an authenticated account.

**Why:** Free participation should remain low-friction, while invites and longitudinal progress need an accountable identity and must not silently attach to a shared or disposable profile.

**How to apply:** Keep paid, personalized, wallet, settlement, invite, user-search, streak, and step-sync paths authenticated. Only the free community join/leave path may use the anonymous identity boundary.
