---
name: Step challenge positioning
description: Product differentiation lessons from researching stake-backed walking challenges.
---

The strongest opportunity is to make the money and verification model easy to understand before commitment, then pair it with social accountability and humane recovery options.

**Why:** Public feedback around the benchmark praises motivation and payout legitimacy but questions fee transparency and has concern around wearable sync reliability.

**How to apply:** Keep fee math, eligibility, settlement timing, supported health sources, and recovery rules visible in the product flow; treat free/social challenges as a complementary funnel to paid challenges.
